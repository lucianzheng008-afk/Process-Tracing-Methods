## Search and access log

For each high-value source target, record repository, collection, query, date, access restrictions, expected coverage, and result.

## Iteration log

Record every theory-building revision, the observation that prompted it, which evidence is discovery material, and the new independent validation test.

## Decision log

Record inclusion/exclusion decisions, disputed interpretations, conclusion downgrades, and the reasons for stopping collection.
