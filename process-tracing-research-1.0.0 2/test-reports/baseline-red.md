# Baseline RED pressure tests

Date: 2026-07-14  
Condition: three agents answered without access to the target `process-tracing-research` Skill.

## Scenario A — strategic speech and duplicated reporting

The response correctly resisted the prompt's suggestion that a public speech followed by policy change was automatically a smoking gun. It also noticed that several articles repeating one unnamed official were not independent corroboration. It nevertheless stopped at prose diagnosis: there were no stable proposition, evidence, source-family, or claim IDs; no explicit access probability for missing private records; and no artifact that another researcher could audit.

## Scenario B — three-country research design

The response produced a plausible mechanism, timelines, alternatives, and a qualitative updating table. The failure was structural rather than intellectual: test labels were not consistently justified against both the focal and best alternative explanation; case-access asymmetry was not carried into the inference; and tables, mechanism steps, and prose lacked shared IDs. A polished answer could therefore drift during later revision without detection.

## Scenario C — polished Falklands narrative under deadline pressure

The response searched real sources and wrote an integrated narrative, but it moved from acknowledged documentary gaps to the conclusion that the “主机制优于竞争性解释.” It described a record as “接近冒烟枪” without an explicit likelihood comparison and leaned partly on post-action statements. This is the decisive RED failure: fluent narrative outran the diagnostic value of the evidence.

## Requirements derived from RED

1. Require shared IDs across mechanism, evidence, timeline, claims, updates, and visuals.
2. Separate theoretical certainty/uniqueness from empirical access, meaning, reliability, and independence.
3. Make source-family dependence auditable; repeated reporting must never be counted as repeated evidence.
4. Require at least one plausible alternative explanation and evidence expected under it.
5. Block strong conclusions when a necessary mechanism step lacks evidence or when a claim has no evidence IDs.
6. Treat test names as optional summaries of likelihood reasoning, not as substitutes for it.
7. Force the final prose to preserve unresolved conflicts and documentary limits.
